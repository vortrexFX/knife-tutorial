01 — Gather your materials

Safety first This tutorial is for a solid, nonfunctional wooden display replica. Keep the blade-shaped section blunt, do not sharpen it, and permanently attach it to the handle. Secure wood before working on it, cut away from your body, and get adult help with saws, drills and unfamiliar tools. Follow the tool and finish manufacturers' safety instructions.

02 — Follow the tutorial

1

Draw your template

Start with a side-view drawing based on your reference picture.

  1. Draw a long handle with a gentle curve.
  2. Sketch a separate, broad, blunt wooden blade-shaped piece.
  3. Mark the positions of the end caps, decorative pins and badge.
  4. Check that all parts fit together on paper before cutting wood.
Tip: A paper template helps keep both sides of the handle symmetrical.
2

Choose and prepare the wood

Choose straight-grained wood without cracks or loose knots in the areas you need.

  1. Inspect the wood for damage.
  2. Transfer your handle template onto the blank.
  3. Mark the separate pieces for the display blade and end caps.
  4. Keep enough extra material to refine the outside edges.
Tip: Basswood is generally easy to carve and is a good choice for beginners.
3

Cut the handle blank

Make the rough outside shape before adding any decoration.

  1. Secure the wood on a stable work surface.
  2. Have an experienced adult help with any saw work.
  3. Remove excess wood gradually around the template.
  4. Leave the handle thick enough to remain strong.
Important: Never hold a small workpiece in your hand while sawing or drilling it.
4

Shape the curved handle

Refine the outline until it resembles the reddish-brown handle in your picture.

  1. Round the outer corners.
  2. Shape the ends so they transition smoothly into the middle.
  3. Refine the gentle curve along the top and bottom.
  4. Check the shape from the side and both faces.
Tip: Remove small amounts at a time. You can always remove more wood, but you cannot put it back easily.
5

Make the blunt wooden blade shape

Make this piece from wood and keep it separate from the handle until it is ready to be attached.

  1. Transfer the curved silhouette from your paper template.
  2. Cut the broad outline with appropriate woodworking tools.
  3. Round the tip and all edges.
  4. Check that the piece is solid, smooth and free from sharp edges.
Design goal: Reproduce the appearance of the reference, not a working blade. Do not create a cutting edge.
6

Add the contrasting end caps

The pale ends create the contrast that makes the original handle recognizable.

  1. Prepare small pieces of light-colored wood.
  2. Shape them to follow the rounded ends of the handle.
  3. Dry-fit the pieces and check for gaps.
  4. Glue them in place and allow the adhesive to cure fully.
Tip: Light natural wood can give you the pale look without metallic materials.
7

Add decorative pins and badge

Create the visual details using contrasting wood.

  1. Mark three small decorative pin positions on the handle.
  2. Use short wooden dowels for the pin details.
  3. Make a small rounded rectangle for the raised badge.
  4. Glue the details securely and let them cure.
Tip: A painted border on the badge can imitate the look of a decorative inset.
8

Attach the wooden pieces

Assemble the display replica only after the parts fit properly.

  1. Check the alignment of the handle and blade-shaped piece.
  2. Make sure the blade-shaped piece has rounded edges and a blunt tip.
  3. Use suitable wood glue to attach it permanently to the handle.
  4. Secure the assembly according to the glue instructions.
  5. Wait for the adhesive to cure fully before continuing.
Important: This is a fixed, nonfunctional display piece, not a folding or cutting tool.
9

Sand and smooth the surfaces

Careful sanding makes a major difference to the final appearance.

  1. Start with a coarser grit only where rough marks need removal.
  2. Move through progressively finer grits, such as 120, 180 and 240.
  3. Follow the wood grain where practical.
  4. Carefully smooth the end caps and raised badge.
  5. Remove sanding dust before finishing.
Tip: Follow the grain rather than sanding across it unnecessarily. Wear suitable dust protection.
10

Stain and finish the wood

Give the handle its rich reddish-brown color.

  1. Test the stain on a scrap of the same wood.
  2. Apply a suitable reddish-brown wood stain according to its label.
  3. Allow the stain to dry as directed.
  4. Apply a compatible clear finish according to its instructions.
  5. Finish the end caps and badge in contrasting colors if desired.
  6. Allow the entire project to cure before handling it.
Final touch: A painted gold-colored badge and naturally pale end caps can create a look similar to your reference photo.

Project complete? 🎉

Check your progress above. Once every step is complete, your wooden display replica should have a curved silhouette, a reddish-brown handle, contrasting ends, decorative pins and a raised badge.

Remember: the blade-shaped part should remain wooden, blunt and permanently attached.
